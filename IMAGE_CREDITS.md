# Ingredient photograph credits

The latest revision replaces 29 photographs with clearly identifiable Wikipedia/Wikimedia Commons ingredient parts and source plants. Selection favours clean specimen framing and higher-resolution photographs, including Featured and Quality images where available. Public-domain, CC0 and credited Creative Commons licences are used; the entire collection is not copyright-free.

Photographs identify source parts or raw materials, not a finished Madhav extract, oil or mineral preparation. Files are unmodified; responsive contain framing or CSS cropping may be used. Enlarged views preserve the complete photograph. Each guide card retains source and licence links.
